---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-17
  Info:
    - Complete rewrite for Factorio 2.0 with a simpler code base. The mod is inspired by the earlier mods
      Extended Research Queue (sh4dow) and Research Queue (sonaxaton).
    - No longer depends on flib.
  Features:
    - Searching technologies now works in the player's own language, not only on internal names.
    - The window shrinks to fit small screens.
    - The science pack filter follows what the force can currently produce until a pack is toggled by hand.
    - The research queue is read back from the vanilla queue through the 2.0 research events.
    - Translations for Danish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian,
      Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
  Changes:
    - The vanilla queue synchronisation setting is replaced by one on/off setting, "Mirror queue to the
      vanilla research queue". When on, the first 4 queue entries are mirrored into the vanilla queue;
      when off, the extended queue is only a planning list.
    - Saves from earlier versions start with an empty extended queue; research already queued in the
      vanilla queue is picked up.
    - The search field is always visible in the title bar.
    - New shortcut bar icon: three research flasks.
    - Technology cells have a fixed width and the lists never scroll horizontally, so the layout cannot
      shift when a horizontal scrollbar would otherwise appear.
    - Technologies unlocked by a research trigger are shown but can no longer be queued.
    - Removed the research completion notification setting, the cheat button and the debug button.
    - Progress bars and time estimates update every second while the window is open.
  Bugfixes:
    - The research speed estimate is now part of the save data. It was kept outside it before, which could
      desynchronise multiplayer games.
    - Adding a technology as next or ASAP now also moves its missing prerequisites to the front.
    - Pausing and emptying the queue in the vanilla UI is now respected instead of being refilled at once.
    - Prerequisite checks between several queued levels of the same infinite technology are now exact.
    - Prepared for Factorio 2.1: science packs are found through technology ingredients and multi-entity
      mining triggers are handled. This is untested.
